# Domain message contracts

Implement immutable typed contracts for time, transforms, twists, stamped values, observations, tracks, grasp candidates, interception plans, cutter state, cell events, and results.

## Acceptance

- Quaternion normalization and finite-number checks are enforced.
- Units appear in field names or types.
- Serialization round trips without information loss.
- Contracts do not depend on Isaac, ROS, NumPy, or wall time.
- Unit tests cover equality, invalid values, and round trips.
