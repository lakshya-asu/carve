# Grasp, hold, damage, and slip model

Implement separate models for capture geometry, frictional holding, contact-pressure damage proxy, and post-grasp slip transform.

## Acceptance

- Increased friction or force cannot reduce holding margin.
- Increased acceleration or mass cannot increase holding margin.
- Increased contact area cannot increase pressure.
- Capture failure is separate from hold failure.
- Slip is logged as a transform and may be corrected by Solution B.
