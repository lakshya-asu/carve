# Physical calibration and OEM asset replacement

Replace reference assumptions with selected OEM robot and gripper assets plus measurements from the real conveyor, cameras, cutter interface, meat samples, and benchtop grasp trials.

## Blockers

- The FANUC M-10iD/12 is selected and its official description is imported as a simulation reference. A real controller interface and physical validation are not available.
- No OEM gripper asset or force calibration is available.
- No camera calibration recording or measured latency distribution is available.
- No meat geometry, friction, compliance, damage, adhesion, or slip dataset is available.
- No guarded cutter PLC timing capture or real safety validation is available.

## Acceptance

- OEM asset provenance and limits are documented.
- Camera intrinsics, extrinsics, exposure timing, and latency are measured.
- Conveyor speed, acceleration, encoder delay, and slip are measured.
- Grasp force, pressure, compliance, friction, damage, and loss models are fitted from trials.
- Cutter permissive and feed timing are replayed from recorded I/O.
- Simulation predictions are compared against held-out physical trials with uncertainty bounds.
- Claims remain limited to the evidence collected.
