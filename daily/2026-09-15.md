- Pipeline piece 1, the depth camera. Lakshya chose to model two cameras and compare them: Orbbec
  Gemini 335L and RealSense D455 (`src/meat_cell_sim/cameras.py`), each from its datasheet
  (resolution, separate horizontal and vertical fields of view, range, 95 mm baseline) with Intel's
  range-squared stereo noise model. MuJoCo 3.12 renders the datasheet lens: a flat marker lands
  within 0.3 px of prediction on both, where the old square-pixel model was 3.5 to 7 px off (5 to
  10 mm on the belt). Experiment record written before the run:
  `experiments/2026-09-15-depth-camera-comparison.md`. 20 legs × 9 poses per camera: depth error
  on the leg 0.94 mm (Gemini) and 0.88 mm (D455) RMS, shank pixels 7,531 and 8,037, legs fully in
  view 158 and 140 of 180. Two defects found and fixed on the way: my scripts placed every leg
  160 mm toward the open edge (outline offset applied along x, not along the leg); and the
  saturation-based glare rule deletes depth across MuJoCo's overexposed white belt, so valid-depth
  figures are a rendering artefact. Follow-up with the cameras turned 90 degrees on the mount
  (long side across the belt): D455 clipped frames 40 to 23, Gemini unchanged at 22, and every
  remaining clipped frame is a leg the harness placed through the far rail and the physics threw
  off the belt (test-pose defect, to fix). Proposed choice for Lakshya: Gemini 335L, long side
  across the belt, on washdown and hardware sync.

- Pipeline step order chosen by Lakshya: segment the leg first, then centre of gravity (geometry
  baseline, then learned), then grasp point and intercept. Every step compares a geometric and a
  learned method, including the compute the learned one needs, and the two are built to run
  together. CPU PyTorch 2.x and scikit-learn 1.9.1 installed into `rll` with Lakshya's approval.
- Step 1, leg segmentation (`src/meat_cell_sim/leg_segmentation.py`, record
  `experiments/2026-09-15-leg-segmentation.md`, written before the run). First run, 180 frames:
  every frame accepted, precision 1.000, but recall fell from 0.999 to 0.934 as legs sat further
  from the rail, centroid error up to 50 mm. Cause found by diagnostic: past the open edge the
  empty-belt reference sees the floor, so the overhanging trotter read as 0.99 m tall and failed
  the 300 mm ceiling. First fix broke every frame: the belt plane fit found the floor, which fills
  more of this wide camera's view than the belt. Fixed by fitting only inside the belt; a test now
  checks the plane is the belt. Run 3: all 180 frames exact (IoU 1.000, centroid 0.003 mm), an
  upper bound because MuJoCo draws a sharp depth edge.
- Lakshya asked for edge effects, a real-footage test, and a page split into segments (camera,
