- Findings that change the plan:
  - Outcome composition (AND, OR, NOT over goals) is exact only with deterministic dynamics and a
    shared goal set. A leg sliding on a wet belt has neither, so orient gets a bound and the saw
    measures the rest. In the composition note's corridor example, averaging two skills'
    Q-functions oscillates forever (return -4.0 over 40 steps); the min over extended values
    walks to the shared goal (return 1.5).
  - No published skill latent carries a precondition or a success test. A latent belongs inside a
    skill as its action head, with the contract checked from outside.
  - Every real zero-shot transfer to an unseen arm uses end-effector poses and that arm's own IK.
    No paper found transfers a policy to a SCARA or any four-axis arm.
  - The cross-embodiment and latent-skill notes disagree on the demo manoeuvre: grasp-and-rotate
    (exists on both arms) against a planar push (inside the SCARA's rating). Turning a gripped
    leg puts the SR-20iA over its 0.45 kg m^2 J4 limit, 1.06x through the centre of gravity and
    4.06x on the shank. Gripping through the centre of gravity is not possible: the ham is about
    250 mm wide and the jaw opens to 180 mm.
  - Next cells by reuse, from the skill-tree note's scoring (design judgment): boxing bagged cuts,
    singulating touching legs, bag loader infeed, ham trimming, hook loading. The footage GPS is
    consistent with Prestage Foods of Iowa near Eagle Grove, Wholestone's joint-venture partner
